\section{System Overview and Architecture}

The proposed system follows four layers: Sensing, Edge Processing,
Communication, and Gateway/Cloud. Two forest nodes monitor the environment
continuously while most hardware components remain in deep sleep to conserve
energy. Nodes wake on a scheduled timer or upon detection of a significant
acoustic or environmental event. Figure~\ref{fig:architecture} shows the
layered architecture of the system.
