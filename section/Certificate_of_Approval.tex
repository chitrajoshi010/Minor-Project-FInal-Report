The undersigned certify that they have read and recommended to the Department
of Electronics and Computer Engineering, IOE, Thapathali Campus, a minor
project work entitled ``Scalable Multi-hop LoRa Networks for Intelligent Forest
Monitoring'' submitted by Chitra Raj Joshi, Keshar Singh Sunar, Prabesh
Parajulee and Santosh Gadtaula in partial fulfillment for the award of
Bachelor's Degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering. The Project
was carried out under special supervision and within the time frame prescribed
by the syllabus.

We found the students to be hardworking, skilled and ready to undertake any
related work to their field of study and hence we recommend the award of
partial fulfillment of Bachelor's degree of Electronics and Communication
Engineering.
